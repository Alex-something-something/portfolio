Project Scope & Leadership

I am leading BURPG’s Argo aerocover design project. The cover is intended to protect the external downcomer pipe and wiring from aerodynamic and thermal loading while reducing the drag associated with the protruding hardware.

The team is developing multiple candidate profiles in parallel, comparing leading edges, midsections, and boattails against the previous Icarus aerocover. Concept selection also considers mounting, composite fabrication, surface finish, and cost.

Current Status

The September 2026 conceptual design review documents candidate geometries and manufacturing approaches. Detailed CAD, simulation, optimization, and validation remain in progress; final performance results will be added as the design develops.

Analysis & Design Plan

  • SolidWorks Design: Model candidate cover profiles and their interfaces with Argo, allowing clearance for the downcomer and wiring.
  • CFD: Characterize flow and loading at Mach 2, 2.5, and 2.81, comparing candidate designs with the baseline.
  • Adjoint CFD: Explore leading-edge optimization while checking the resulting geometry against physical and manufacturing constraints.
  • FEA: Use aerodynamic loads from CFD to evaluate structural response.
  • Flight Performance: Investigate the effect of aerocover drag using OpenRocket.

Manufacturing & Integration

The design review considers composite wet layup over 3D-printed or machined foam molds, with attention to surface finish, dimensional control, and material cost. Mounting concepts address protruding fasteners and their aerodynamic effects.

Open work includes resin temperature limits, mounting feasibility, layup trials, and practical validation methods. These studies will inform the final geometry and manufacturing process.

Project context: Aerocover Design Doc and Argo Aerocover CoDR, September 3, 2026. Candidate concepts and analysis plans are preliminary.
