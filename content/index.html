February–May 2025 · BURPG

Developed assembly and mounting designs in SolidWorks for an existing suppression-system concept, including 3D-printed adapters, mounting hardware, and sheet-metal fixtures for the VEGAS stand.

  • Planned fabrication and pipe routing with pipe-bending guidance.
