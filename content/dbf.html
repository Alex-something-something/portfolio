Contribution & Results

I owned the Prototype 1 wing’s structure and CAD, then developed a proposed split-wing Prototype 2 using lessons from the build. Prototype 2 was not manufactured.

Prototype 1

Trade studies on structure, material, and manufacturing led to an XPS foam wing that informed the Prototype 2 concept. I hot-wire cut the foam sections and assembled them with a carbon-fiber main spar, smaller spars to resist torsion, a 3D-printed center section, and printed aileron servo boxes.

  • Complete-wing comparison: Prototype 1 was 42.7% lighter than the prior balsa/film wing, including spars, center section, servos, and hardware in both assemblies.
  • Build time: Reduced elapsed wing build time from three months to one through material and manufacturing trades.
  • Assembly & Serviceability: Designed a printed PLA center section with 4-40 spar-retention screws and access holes to check carbon-fiber spar alignment and epoxy placement. Taped-over wiring channels retained the airfoil surface while routing servo leads to the fuselage.
  • Control Integration: Integrated printed PLA servo boxes and control rods, laser-cut control horns, and tape-hinged XPS ailerons. The review documented separate servo-lid and spar-attachment holes so actuation hardware could be located and secured during assembly.
  • Technical Communication: Presented multiple design reviews covering the new wings, supporting trade studies, and manufacturing processes.

Prototype 2 & Leadership

For the proposed second prototype, I designed a split-wing NACA 2412 configuration in Fusion 360 using aerodynamic-subteam specifications alongside manufacturing and accessibility lessons from Prototype 1. The November one-pager specifies 10 mm OD carbon-fiber spars joined by 0.5 in OD 6061-T6 aluminum tubes bent to the 5° dihedral, with M3 retention holes. Printed ABS interfaces and servo boxes, partial balsa ribs, and a fuselage-matching root cutout address assembly and surface support. The elevator and half-wing aileron annotations are options under consideration, not demonstrated features.

  • Planform: 0.52 taper ratio
  • Dihedral: 5°
  • Servo Flap System: Designed the servo flap system as part of the wing-lead work.
  • Technical Mentorship: Mentored nine members in laser cutting, waterjet operation, and Fusion 360.
  • Leadership: Mentored the upcoming wing lead in manufacturing logistics and design-for-manufacture principles.

Earlier Aircraft: 2024–2025 Competition Build

Before the two wing iterations described above, I contributed to fabrication of the 8.2 lb competition aircraft: a fiberglass composite fuselage, balsa airframe, and Clark-Y wing. My work focused on composite layup, manufacturing, and detailed CAD of the wing’s internal structure.

For this earlier aircraft, I also authored mission analysis and aircraft-design justifications for the national competition report.
