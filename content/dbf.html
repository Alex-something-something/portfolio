Contribution & Results

As Wing Structural Lead, I led the structural design and CAD development of two iterations of Boston University AIAA Design-Build-Fly's flagship aircraft. Through material, structural, and manufacturing trade studies, the first XPS foam prototype wing was 50% lighter than the previous year's wing, with build time reduced from three months to one.

Prototype 1

I conducted trade studies and presented a design review on structural configuration, material selection, and manufacturing method. The resulting XPS foam wing established a lighter, faster-to-build baseline for the next iteration.

  • Mass Reduction: 50% lighter XPS foam prototype wing than the previous year's aircraft.
  • Manufacturing Throughput: Reduced wing build time from three months to one, a 67% reduction, through design and process improvements.
  • Technical Communication: Presented multiple design reviews covering the new wings, supporting trade studies, and manufacturing processes.

Prototype 2 & Leadership

For the second prototype, I designed a split-wing NACA 2412 configuration in Fusion 360 using aerodynamic-subteam specifications alongside manufacturing and accessibility lessons from Prototype 1.

  • Planform: 0.52 taper ratio
  • Dihedral: 5°
  • Winglets: 4° vertical winglets
  • Servo Flap System: Designed the servo flap system as part of the wing-lead work.
  • Technical Mentorship: Mentored nine members in laser cutting, waterjet operation, and Fusion 360.
  • Leadership: Mentored the upcoming wing lead in manufacturing logistics and design-for-manufacture principles.

Earlier Aircraft: 2024–2025 Competition Build

Before the two wing iterations described above, I contributed to fabrication of the 8.2 lb competition aircraft: a fiberglass composite fuselage, balsa airframe, and Clark-Y wing. My work focused on composite layup, manufacturing, and detailed CAD of the wing’s internal structure.

For this earlier aircraft, I also authored mission analysis and aircraft-design justifications for the national competition report.
